A FEW GREEN LIGHTS

Worth knowing.

What do we receive, and when?

One week after we install our cameras, you receive your junction analysis and an optimized traffic-light cycle proposal. The report explains observed lane directions, vehicle volumes, and turning movements, alongside the proposed timing plan.

What does the city need to provide?

No cameras, equipment purchases, or IT integration. We bring and install our cameras, collect the footage, and handle the analysis. Site access and any necessary installation permissions are agreed before we begin.

How do you keep the service low-cost?

We bring our own cameras and handle the study as one service. Your city does not need to buy equipment or undertake an IT integration project to receive the analysis. We scope the service around the junction you want to understand.

Does ramzor automatically change the lights?

We deliver the analysis and a proposed optimized traffic-light cycle. Applying it to a live junction is subject to the city's approval and controller requirements. The service is not an automatic signal-control system.
