03 HOW THE PILOT WORKS

You choose the junction.
We handle the rest.

A managed path from one intersection to an engineering report and a reviewed signal-cycle proposal.

  1. 01

    Choose.

    The municipality identifies one intersection it wants to understand or improve.

  2. 02

    Install.

    ramzor.io brings and installs temporary cameras. No municipal equipment or IT integration is required.

  3. 03

    Observe.

    We observe traffic for roughly one week and process video solely for traffic measurement.

  4. 04

    Analyze.

    We measure movements, volumes, queues, and demand patterns across the junction.

  5. 05

    Report.

    You receive an engineering report and a proposed traffic-light cycle with the evidence behind it.

  6. 06

    Optimize the cycle.

    We turn the findings into an optimized traffic-light cycle and handle the work needed to put the approved timing into operation.

ramzor.io handles the entire study. Cameras, installation, observation, analysis, reporting, and the proposed cycle are all taken care of. No equipment, software, IT integration, fieldwork, or analysis is required from your team.

PRIVACY

Traffic analysis,
not surveillance.

Our cameras are used to measure traffic behavior—not to identify people. ramzor.io follows GDPR principles of purpose limitation, data minimization, and limited retention.

  • No facial recognition. We do not identify pedestrians, drivers, or passengers.
  • No license-plate identification. Registration numbers are not read or linked to traffic records.
  • Traffic measurement only. Video is processed to produce counts, movements, queues, and timing evidence.
  • Defined retention. Raw video is kept only while the study is analyzed and verified, then deleted. Only non-identifying traffic measurements and the final report are retained.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few practical questions.

Does one week include the report?

One week refers to the observation period. Analysis and the signal-timing proposal follow. We agree the observation schedule and report delivery date with you before installation.

Do we need to manage any part of the study?

No. Tell us which junction you want to understand and ramzor.io handles the cameras, installation, observation, analysis, engineering report, and proposed traffic-light cycle. No equipment, software, IT integration, fieldwork, or analysis is required from your team.

Will ramzor.io change the traffic lights?

We can arrange and set the new signal cycle once your team approves the proposal, subject to the agreed scope and controller requirements. Nothing changes automatically or without city approval.

Can you measure queues at any intersection?

Camera position, obstructions, and the visible length of each lane matter. We review the site and measurement limits before agreeing what the study can cover.

How is camera footage handled?

Video is used only to measure traffic. ramzor.io does not use facial recognition or license-plate identification. Raw video is kept only while the study is analyzed and verified, then deleted. Only non-identifying traffic measurements and the final report are retained.
